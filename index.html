P2P LENDING MECHANISM

How P2P lending works

P2P Lending transactions are facilitated through Trustee Backed Escrow Accounts.

1
Transfers MoneyLender deposits funds in the escrow account.
2
Loans DisbursedFunds are disbursed to approved borrowers.
3
Repayment to EscrowLoan repayments are transferred to the escrow account.
4
Funds Released to LendersLoan repayments are settled directly to the lender’s bank account.
Repayments received through the escrow account are settled directly to the lender’s bank account.
Simple flow

Lender → Escrow Account → Approved Borrowers → Escrow Account → Lender’s bank account

How to read the numbers

We use standard financial terms where they are useful, and explain them in plain English so users can understand exactly what each metric means.

Realized return

Historical return earned on completed portfolios during the stated period. It is not a future-return promise.

APR

Annual Percentage Rate. It describes the annualized rate applicable to the lending arrangement.

GNPA

Gross Non-Performing Assets. It measures the principal outstanding in loans classified as non-performing under the applicable methodology.

DPD

Days Past Due. It tells you how many days a scheduled repayment has remained unpaid.

Realized loss

The amount ultimately borne as a loss by lenders after the applicable recovery process.

Recovery rate

The percentage of the relevant defaulted principal that has been recovered.
