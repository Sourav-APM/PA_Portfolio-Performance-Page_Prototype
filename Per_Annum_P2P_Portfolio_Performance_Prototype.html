How we calculate the numbers

Final production definitions should remain aligned with the applicable RBI framework and verified underlying portfolio data.

Realized return

Return calculated from completed portfolios for the stated period. It is not a guaranteed future return.

GNPA

Gross non-performing amount based on principal outstanding for loans classified as NPA under the applicable methodology.

DPD

Days Past Due. Ageing buckets show how long repayments have remained overdue.

Realized loss

Principal and/or interest amounts ultimately borne by lenders after applicable recovery.

Recovery rate

Recovered defaulted principal divided by the relevant defaulted principal base.

APR

Annual Percentage Rate used when communicating the applicable lending rate.
